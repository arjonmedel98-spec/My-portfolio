About

I started at eClerx handling real-time website modifications — live edits and troubleshooting based on client requests over the phone, where accuracy and responsiveness weren't optional. That hands-on stretch as Senior Web Designer became the foundation for a Team Lead role I've held since 2023, where I now guide a team of web designers, distribute the work, and coach people on it, while still building things myself.

I care about the same thing on every project, whether it's a five-page Duda site, a pitch deck, or a custom system: does it actually make the person using it faster and less confused. That's the question behind the Gym Management System below, and behind most of the work in the grid underneath it.

Featured project

Gym Management System — a custom web app, built end to end

Gyms I worked with were tracking memberships and payments by hand. I designed and built a web-based system that watches membership status, generates invoices, processes payments, and handles renewals — so the front desk runs off one dashboard instead of a stack of spreadsheets.
